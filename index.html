Known Issues — BMW M2 F87 (N55) 6 issues
High Charge pipe cracking £80–£200

The OEM plastic charge pipe is known to split under boost pressure, causing sudden loss of power and limp mode. Very common on N55 M2s — an upgraded silicone pipe is a recommended preventive fix.

Medium Oil leaks — valve cover & oil filter housing gaskets £300–£600

Rubber gaskets harden from heat cycling and begin leaking — typically from the valve cover and oil filter housing. Oil can reach the exhaust, creating a fire risk. Both are usually done together to save on labour.

Medium Electric water pump failure £400–£700

The electric water pump can fail without warning, causing rapid overheating. Unlike a mechanical pump, there's no gradual warning — it simply stops. Most common between 60k–100k miles. Replace proactively if near that range.

Medium HPFP failure (high-pressure fuel pump) £500–£900

High-pressure fuel pump failures have been reported on high-mileage or previously tuned N55 engines, leading to rough running, misfires, and hard starts. Check for any history of tuning before buying.

Low Ignition coil misfires £30–£120

Failing ignition coils cause misfires, rough idle, and loss of power. Relatively cheap and easy to fix — usually resolved by replacing the affected coil(s). A full set replacement is often recommended at high mileage.

Low VANOS solenoid fault £150–£350

VANOS (variable valve timing) solenoid failures can cause rough idle, hesitation, and loss of low-end power. Often triggers a fault code before becoming a major issue — check for any stored codes before purchasing.

✓ Overall verdict

The N55-powered M2 is the most reliable F87 variant. When properly maintained and kept near stock, it's genuinely dependable. The charge pipe and water pump are the two proactive fixes worth doing regardless of mileage.
